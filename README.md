# Interactive dashboard using MS Excel

This shows the effect of drugs A, B, C, X and Y on 200 patients. Analysis was carried out to check the drug that might be most accurate in managing patients. Age, Sex, BP and Cholesterol level and Na-to-K ratio were given to check the efficacy of these drugs.

## Project Objective:
To observe the effect of experimental drugs on the blood pressure, cholesterol level and Na-K ratio on 200 patients. Parameters taken into account are sex and ages of these patients.

## Process:
- Data verified for inconsistent values and anomalies, and sorted them out
- Made sure data is clean with respect to data type and data format
- Created pivot tables to exploratory analysis

## Data Insights: 
- Cholesterol is approximately at the same levels, given that the male-female ratio is 52:48. The male patients have slightly higher cholesterol rates making this parameter indeterminate.
- Blood pressure levels are lowest in males between 40-50 while the highest levels are found in women aged 20-40.
- Na to K Ratio - The Drugs A,B,C, and X kept patients Na-K ranges below the range 10-20, which is way below standard. Only Drug Y show potential at accuracy in managing patients' electrolyte levels.
- Blood Pressure - Drug Y is found in all 3 categories, obscuring its potential as an accurate therapy, X shows additional potential as it provides a higher rate of patients with a normal blood pressure.
- Cholesterol - Y is found in highest levels in both HIGH and NORMAL categories, making Drug X a better alternative in managing patients' cholesterol.

## Conclusion:
Drug Y has a high chance at providing accuracy in managing patients as it positions most patients on it with a balanced Na-to-K, however it fails to effectively manage Blood pressure and cholesterol levels. Drug X provides a better alternative at Blood pressure and Cholesterol regulation, making it a better option at obtaining accuracy.
